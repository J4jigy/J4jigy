<product_requirements>
The application is a comprehensive financial management tool for petrol pump businesses. The primary goal is to provide a robust Point of Sale (POS) system combined with financial tracking and management features.

**Core Features Implemented:**
-   **POS System ():** A multi-slot POS interface for managing concurrent sales. It supports invoice, challan, and gate pass generation with print previews. Functionality includes swipe and double-click gestures for quick access to these documents.
-   **Financial Tracking:** Components for managing cash in/out, rent (given/received), customers/debtors, and suppliers/creditors.
-   **Dashboard:** A central hub with tiles for navigating to different features like Challan / Gate Pass, Stock Management, and Staff.
-   **UI/UX:** A focus on a modern, mobile-friendly interface with floating action buttons for adding new entries, animated pop-ups, and a sliding profile drawer for settings and navigation.
-   **Customer/Vendor Management:** Modals for selecting customers and vendors, with categories and sub-categories to open user-added contact lists.
