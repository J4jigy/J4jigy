<product_requirements>
The application is a financial management dashboard featuring a dark theme, a 3x4 grid, summary sections (You will Give, You will Receive), and quick-switch tabs (Business, Finance, Personal) with embossed tiles, colored icons, and consistent text. Essential features include prominent Cash In (Green) and Cash Out (Red) buttons, invite-only login, user registration, a secure backend with an admin panel, and real-time double-entry accounting supporting multi-user logins. UI enhancements included detailed Add Cash In/Out Entry screens with a functional calculator, barcode scanning, various payment modes, quick amount buttons (initially 1-500, with specific size/placement, later evolving through multiple iterations of icon-based designs, sounds, and final button-based green styling), and a POS-style multi-customer billing system (C1-C6 slots with independent amounts, badges, local storage, quantity controls). Modals for product/expense selection required quantity controls and measurement selectors. All screens must fit the viewport without scrolling. Feature tiles navigate to dedicated full-page list views with search, sort, and pagination. Recent additions include a business switcher, To-Do list, credit terms dropdown, floating chat, and peer-to-peer chat with onboarded peers.
</product_requirements>
