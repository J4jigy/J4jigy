<product_requirements>
The application is a web-based financial management tool for petrol pumps, designed to manage operations from an empty state with dynamic data.
**Key Features & Implemented Solutions:**
*   **Data Management & Persistence**: User-managed data for products, expenses, cash entries, credit sales, DT Plus, Lubes, staff, and payroll. Data persists via local storage for business-specific data, with future cloud sync. Hardcoded data was removed from , , .
*   **Dashboard & Navigation**: Dashboard displays feature tiles across Business, Finance, and Personal tabs, remembering the last active tab. Back arrows now use explicit route navigation.
*   **Cash Management**: A Cash section (Finance tab) includes search, sorting, pagination, and a Current Cash Balance card.
*   **Fuel Dispenser Details**: Dynamic subsections for various entries with add/delete and total calculations.
*   **Multi-Business Data Isolation**: Separate data records per business, with import/export functionality.
*   **Role-Based Staff Management**: Multiple staff users with roles (Owner, Manager, Cashier, Accountant, Staff). Features staff invitation (email, WhatsApp), role assignment, and permission-based UI access.
*   **Payroll Management**: Under staff management, includes staff salary, attendance, and payment history with Indian Rupee symbols.
*   **Financial Reporting**: Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, Daily Sales Report, Community Ratings, Total Payables (You Will Give), and Total Receivables (You Will Receive) pages have been implemented with enhanced designs, period comparisons, and detailed breakdowns matching user screenshots.
*   **UI/UX**: Clean, responsive design with compact layouts. An attempt to add embossed styling was made but later reverted to the original clean design.
</product_requirements>
